# 80-ideas

## Purpose
Future ideas, text only, no prototypes.

## What goes here
- `IDEAS.md`: one section per idea, with date and context.

## What does NOT go here
- Prototypes or mocks (`70-proposals/`).

## Conventions
- Section heading: `## YYYY-MM-DD: <idea title>`.

## When to delete
- Preserve `IDEAS.md` across plans. During planning, list ideas with explanations, consequences and recommendations, and ask which to include.
- A selected idea leaves this file only once captured in an approved plan or proposal; deferred ideas stay. Never silently discard ideas.
