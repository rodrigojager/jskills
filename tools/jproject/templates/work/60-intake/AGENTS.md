# 60-intake

## Purpose
Material from outside the container that was already accepted and must be adapted and integrated. Question: how do we integrate this?

## What goes here
- One `<name>/` folder per intake, with `INTAKE.md` (create with `jproject new intake <name>`) and the files as they arrived.
- From a parallel folder: its `out/` plus its `HANDOFF.md` renamed to `INTAKE.md`.

## What does NOT go here
- Things generated here awaiting a decision (`70-proposals/`).
- Material without a defined destination.

## Conventions
- If an intake arrives without `INTAKE.md`, the agent writes one from what the user says.
- `INTAKE.md` status: `pending`, `integrating`, `done`.

## When to delete
- As soon as it is integrated. The original parallel folder is deleted only with the user's confirmation.
