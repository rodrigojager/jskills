# 70-proposals

## Purpose
Things generated inside this project that await a decision. Question: does it go in or not?

## What goes here
- One `<name>/` folder per proposal, with `PROPOSAL.md` (create with `jproject new proposal <name>`) and its mocks/notes.

## What does NOT go here
- Ideas with no work done (`80-ideas/IDEAS.md`).
- Material from outside (`60-intake/`).

## Conventions
- `PROPOSAL.md` status: `in-progress`, then `awaiting-review`.
- Open proposals are listed in Active items of `WORKSPACE.md` (`jproject map`).

## When to delete
- Integrated: apply it and delete the folder. Rejected: delete without a trace, unless the user asks to keep it.
