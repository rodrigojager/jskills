# _work

## Purpose
Drafts, logs and tests of this parallel folder.

## What goes here
- Anything that is not the deliverable.

## What does NOT go here
- The deliverable (`out/`).

## Conventions
- One canonical file per artifact; no `_v2`, `_final` or copies.

## When to delete
- Discarded when the deliverable is moved to the origin's intake.
